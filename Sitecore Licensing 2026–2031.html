Breakdown

Products and services AWS has with Sitecore

What each product is, what it does for aws.org, and the capacity we're entitled to.

Experience Manager (XM 10.2)

The content management system behind aws.org. Editors build and publish pages here. JSS (JavaScript Services) lets the front end be built as a headless JavaScript app that pulls content from XM. SXA (Sitecore Experience Accelerator) provides reusable page templates and components so the team can assemble pages without new development.

Architecture

How the Sitecore ecosystem is connected

Content Hub is the source for media and editorial content, XM assembles and publishes the pages, Personalize decides what each visitor sees, and Coveo makes all of it searchable.

1
DAM → XM: every image and documentAll images on aws.org are served from the Content Hub DAM. The documents we display (Welding Journal issues, Welding Research papers, Spraytime and other PDFs) also live in Content Hub and are linked into XM pages, so an asset updated once updates everywhere it's used.
2
CMP → XM: magazine and media workflowEach article, blog post, press release and research paper is written and approved in CMP. When it's ready, the matching content item is created automatically in XM and published on the website, with no copy-and-paste between systems.
3
XM → aws.org: page publishingXM assembles pages from SXA components and Content Hub content, and the JSS front end renders them for visitors. Managed Cloud hosts the production and non-production environments.
4
Personalize ↔ XM: experiences and A/B testsPersonalize tracks visitor behavior (up to 200k MTUs) and runs Sitecore Experiences and Experiments. It decides which variant of a component or page each visitor sees, and XM delivers it.
5
XM → Coveo: page indexingCoveo crawls the XM site content so every page is searchable.
6
Content Hub → Coveo: document indexingCoveo also indexes the documents and assets in Content Hub, so journals and research papers appear in search results alongside pages.
7
Coveo → aws.org: site searchCoveo powers the search experience on the site. The current Coveo term runs until Feb 2029 at $34,848/yr. We plan to move to Coveo Gen AI (Enterprise edition with Relevance Generative Answering, about $99,360/yr plus a $75,875 implementation).
◆
Sitecore 360: support and educationPremium 24×7 support covers the stack, and the Sitecore 360 Education Platform in our contract trains the team on XM, Content Hub and Personalize.
Why this matters for any upgrade or replacement

Every option below has to preserve these seven connections. Upgrading XM (10.4/10.5) keeps the architecture intact. Moving to SitecoreAI requires rebuilding the front end in Next.js and re-wiring the Content Hub, Personalize and search integrations. Replacing Sitecore with another DXP would mean rebuilding all of them.

Support lifecycle

Where XM 10.2 stands

Per Sitecore's product support lifecycle. Effective June 1, 2026, production incident help and security patches for 10.2 are no longer included; under Extended Support they're available at extra cost. In the past year AWS paid nothing extra for defects or patches.

Summary & recommendation

Stay on 10.2 through 2027, then move

The recommendation is to remain on XM 10.2 and renew Extended Support for about 18 months (one more year plus six months, to 31 Dec 2027). This keeps today's level of support and gives time to plan and budget a migration without immediate pressure. A full upgrade needs significant AWS staff time and budget; staying put avoids that in the short term.

Jun – Jul 2026

Renew current contracts. Content Hub (Jun 12), Extended Support (Jun 8), XM bundle (Jul 18). Total $492,023. Sitecore to confirm the 18-month Extended Support option; a 3-year renewal is also being priced.

Early 2028

Feb 2029

Current Coveo term ends. Finishing the Coveo lifecycle is part of the 18-month approach.

2029

New platform fully implemented. Possible 5-year SitecoreAI term using Sitecore Search, subject to how complex it is to migrate our current indexing and components.

What we're still watching

The lifecycle and support dates for XM 10.5 (not yet announced), how SitecoreAI's capabilities evolve, and whether Sitecore Search reaches generative-answer parity with Coveo. Long term, SitecoreAI with Sitecore Search could save money by dropping Coveo, but only if we don't lose the value of the Coveo investment. If AWS upgrades to 10.4 instead, Extended Support would need to be budgeted each year (available through Dec 2030).
